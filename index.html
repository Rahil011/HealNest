About Manali

Thoughtful counselling, backed by rigorous training.

I'm Manali Rawat, a counselling psychologist offering ethical, evidence-informed support in a space that feels safe — not clinical. My approach combines academic depth with warmth, so you can speak openly without judgement.

M.A. in Psychology (Silver Medallist), Integral University, Lucknow. Internships at Rainbow and Astha Centre, Lucknow. Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Psychology, with ongoing research and practice in mental health and wellbeing.

The Heal Nest is my independent practice: a place to pause, reflect, and rebuild — whether you are facing anxiety, relationship difficulty, academic pressure, low self-worth, or the steady weight of everyday stress.

What I help with

Support for what you're carrying right now.

Each session is tailored to you — informed by professional training, shaped by your story. Common areas of focus include:

Anxiety & overthinking

Practical, grounded tools to quiet a racing mind and feel steadier in your body and thoughts.

Relationships

Understanding patterns in how you connect — with partners, family, or friends — and rebuilding trust.

Self-esteem & identity

Working through self-criticism and reconnecting with a more compassionate sense of who you are.

Academic & career stress

For students and professionals navigating pressure, burnout, and big life-direction decisions.

Low mood

Gentle, structured support for the days that feel heavier, without rushing you toward "fixed."

Life transitions

Support through change — new cities, new roles, new chapters — when the ground feels unfamiliar.

How it works

Coming to The Heal Nest, from first message to ongoing care.

first

You reach out

A short message — no long forms about your whole life history yet. Just enough to find a time that works.

then

An intro session

A relaxed first conversation to understand what's going on and whether we're a good fit to work together.

ongoing

Weekly sessions

Regular 50-minute sessions, online or in person, at a pace that respects where you actually are.
